# Web Policy — Aodı Akdıro Web OS

Adopt, sign, then activate into the **🛡️ Web Policy** module checklist (the same 18 items rated high / med / low).

**Ground rule:** approval first, activation second; rejections documented with a reason.

## Trust and legal (high risk)
1. TLS active with automatic renewal, verified before expiry.
2. Written privacy policy visible before any data request.
3. Explicit consent for every marketing email; one-click unsubscribe.
4. Encrypted disks / accounts with managed access — no shared logins.
5. Backups weekly with a tested restore procedure.
6. Platform and plugin updates on a published schedule, not ad hoc.

## Commerce (high/med)
7. Written refund and shipping policy before selling anything.
8. Real product images you own — nothing scraped without license.
9. A working payment tested end-to-end with a real order.
10. Contact page with real response times and channels.
11. Robot-proofed forms with dated verification notes.
12. Prices and checkout totals tested in two currencies if applicable.

## Performance and operations (med/low)
13. Mobile experience verified on a real device before publish.
14. Uptime monitoring from multiple regions with an alert channel.
15. Test environment separated from production.
16. Speed budget: scripts and widgets capped per page.
17. Incident escalation with a channel and a time window.
18. Quarterly policy review with signed changes.
